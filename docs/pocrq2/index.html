RQ2 proofs of concept: defenses that protect APIs, not information

Minimal pages behind RQ2 of the paper Slicing to the Roots. Each page prints the exact code it runs and only shows values on the page; nothing is sent anywhere. Open a page in Chromium or Chrome first, copy the readings, then open it in Brave (Shields at their default) on the same machine and paste them to compare.

What we found

61 APIs read information a Brave defense protects. In Brave's default (Standard) level, the hook sits on the path of 28: they are protected. The other 33 reach the same information without passing a hook that acts at Standard, so they circumvent the defense; in Brave 1.92.144, 29 of the 33 returned exactly the value Brave returns with fingerprinting protection off (Brave 1.97.59: the same counts). The rest: Screen.width and Screen.availWidth were rewritten after all, OffscreenCanvas.transferToImageBitmap leaks by one of its three read-back routes, and one could not be checked.
