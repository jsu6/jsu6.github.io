Audio: Brave protects AudioBuffer reads, not the rendered samples

Audio fingerprinting renders a short sound offline and sums its samples, which differ slightly between machines. Brave adds per-site noise where a page reads the samples from an AudioBuffer (getChannelData, copyFromChannel). Our analysis finds that OfflineAudioContext.startRendering reaches a source Brave's audio defense protects without passing those hooks. That shared source is a configuration flag (the render quantum setting), not the samples themselves: the samples are computed in the renderer and have no source of their own. So this page tests the consequence directly: played into a second offline context, an AudioWorklet reads the rendered samples without calling an AudioBuffer method.
