Audio: Brave protects AudioBuffer reads, not the rendered samples

Audio fingerprinting renders a short sound offline and sums its samples, which differ slightly between machines. Brave adds per-site noise where a page reads the samples from an AudioBuffer (getChannelData, copyFromChannel). Our analysis shows that the samples OfflineAudioContext.startRendering produces reach a page by another path: played into a second offline context, an AudioWorklet reads them directly, without passing those hooks.
