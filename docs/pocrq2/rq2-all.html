Every reader that circumvents a Brave defense, without a permission

Our analysis predicts that 226 APIs reach information a Brave defense protects without passing a hook that acts at Brave's default level. This page runs the lab's probe on the of them that a page reads without a permission or a browser prompt. Here the ground truth is Brave itself with fingerprinting protection off: the same browser, so only the defense differs (Brave also differs from Chromium in ways no defense causes: its brands, codecs and window size). Next to each value the page shows what our lab saw. Nothing leaves this page.

How to check

  1. In Brave, open the Shields panel for this site and turn fingerprinting protection off (Shields → Block fingerprinting → off), reload, click Run. When asked, move the mouse and scroll the wheel over the page (the wheel and mouse readers need an event); the run takes about 30 s. Click Copy readings.
  2. Turn fingerprinting protection back to its default (Standard), reload, click Run, paste the readings and click Compare.
  3. A row our lab graded leaked should read the same with and without protection. rewritten rows should differ; Brave farbles some of them per site, so on one site a value can match (the lab column lists one value per site). For partly rows, the first value (read through getImageData) is farbled and the second (read through WebGL) leaks.
